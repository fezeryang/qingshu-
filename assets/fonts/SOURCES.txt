kuaile/  ZCOOL KuaiLe  (站酷快乐体)  https://github.com/googlefonts/zcool-kuaile
         Used as 'QS KuaiLe': logo, headings, buttons, dialogue, HUD numbers.
hand/    Long Cang     (龙藏体)      https://github.com/googlefonts/longcang
         Used as 'QS Hand': handwritten letter text, sticky notes, pencil annotations.

Both are the unicode-range sliced WOFF2 builds served by Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com), downloaded
once and served locally. The browser only fetches the slices that contain characters on screen.
The font family names were changed in the @font-face rules only (the font files are unmodified).
